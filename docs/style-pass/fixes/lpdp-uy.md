### professionalLayer

**Before**

> Law 18,331 declares data protection an inherent human right and applies broadly to personal data recorded in any medium, across the public and private sectors. Law 19,670 of 2018 extended territorial scope to foreign controllers that offer goods or services to people in Uruguay or analyse their behaviour. It also reaches foreign controllers using means of processing located in Uruguay, or bound by contract or international law. The same law added data protection officers for certain entities, impact assessments, and breach notification. Decree 64/020 implements these duties. Uruguay holds an EU adequacy decision granted in 2012 and became the first non-European state to join Convention 108, in 2013. It ratified the modernised version, known as 108+, in 2021. The URCDP, the national regulatory and control unit, supervises and enforces the regime.

## Your Right to Correct or Delete Your Data in Uruguay (`lpdp-uy-rectification`)

### personalImpact

**Before**

> A mistaken entry in a credit or commercial database can quietly cost you. It may block financing you should qualify for. This law lets you correct or delete inaccurate data. Decisions about you then rest on accurate records.

**After**

> A mistaken entry in a credit or commercial database can quietly cost you. It may block financing you should qualify for. This law lets you correct or delete inaccurate data.

### professionalLayer

**Before**

> Article 15 obliges the controller to rectify, update, include, or suppress personal data upon request, performing the operation within five business days and informing the requester. Refusals must be founded and communicated within the same period. Where data has been transferred to third parties, the controller must notify them of the correction or suppression within the same timeframe so downstream records change too. The habeas data action backs the right where deadlines lapse or refusals lack basis.

**After**

> Article 15 obliges the controller to rectify, update, include, or suppress personal data upon request, performing the operation within five business days and informing the requester. Refusals must give reasons and be communicated within the same period. Where data has been transferred to third parties, the controller must notify them of the correction or suppression within the same timeframe so downstream records change too. The habeas data action backs the right where deadlines lapse or refusals lack basis.

### responseTimeline

**Before**

> 5 business days to perform the operation or communicate a founded refusal.

**After**

> five business days to perform the operation or communicate a refusal with reasons.

## What Happens When Your Data Is Compromised in Uruguay (`lpdp-uy-breach`)

### personalImpact

**Before**

> A leak can expose your cedula number and financial details to fraud. A company that stays silent only widens the damage. Uruguayan rules require notice to the URCDP when your data is at risk. You learn in time to protect yourself.

**After**

> A leak can expose your cédula number and financial details to fraud. A company that stays silent only widens the damage. Uruguayan rules require notice to the URCDP when your data is at risk.

### professionalLayer

**Before**

> Uruguay added a mandatory breach regime through its accountability reforms and Decree 64/020. Upon becoming aware of a security incident affecting personal data, the controller must notify the URCDP within a maximum of 72 hours, start containment within 24 hours, and immediately inform data subjects who suffered a significant impact, in clear and simple language. After resolution, the controller must send the URCDP a detailed report on the incident and measures taken. The structure resembles GDPR's dual-notification model with the 72-hour clock applied to the regulator.

**After**

> Uruguay added a mandatory breach regime through its accountability reforms and Decree 64/020. Upon becoming aware of a security incident affecting personal data, the controller must notify the URCDP within a maximum of 72 hours, start containment within 24 hours, and immediately inform data subjects who suffered a significant impact, in clear and simple language. After resolution, the controller must send the URCDP a detailed report on the incident and measures taken. The structure resembles the GDPR's dual-notification model with the 72-hour clock applied to the regulator.

## Moving Personal Data Out of Uruguay (`lpdp-uy-transfers`)

### personalImpact

**Before**

> Your data may travel to servers in other countries. Protection abroad is not always as strong. This law permits the transfer only with adequate safeguards or your consent. Your rights follow the data across the border.

**After**

> Your data may travel to servers in other countries. Protection abroad is not always as strong. This law permits the transfer only with adequate safeguards or your consent.

### professionalLayer

**Before**

> Article 23 prohibits transfers to countries or international organizations that do not provide adequate levels of protection under international or regional law standards. Exceptions include international judicial cooperation, medical data exchange the person's treatment requires, banking and stock transfers, treaty-based transfers, and intelligence cooperation against serious crime. Others cover the data subject's consent, contracts, public interest or legal claims, vital interests, and public registers. Transfers based on contract clauses or other guarantees need prior URCDP authorisation. The URCDP has recommended model clause sets by resolution, most recently the Council of Europe's clauses in 2026, and adequacy assessments broadly track the European map. Uruguay's own EU adequacy status keeps inbound EU flows unrestricted and anchors its adequacy-first architecture.

**After**

> Article 23 prohibits transfers to countries or international organizations that do not provide adequate levels of protection under international or regional law standards.
